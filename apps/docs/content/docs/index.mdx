---
title: What is Pocket
description: Give each agent its own wallet and a hard cap it cannot raise.
---

Your agents need to buy things. Search results, market data, compute, other
agents' output. Most of that is sold per call, and none of it will take an
invoice from a language model.

So you have a choice. Hand your agent a card and hope, or give it an account
that cannot be talked into anything.

**Pocket is the second option.** Your agent gets its own wallet, its own budget,
and limits it cannot argue its way past. It buys what it needs. You get a
receipt for every attempt, including the ones you refused.

## The idea in one line

The order matters. Pocket decides **before** anything is signed, so a refused
purchase has no signature attached to it. There is nothing sitting around that a
clever prompt could talk an agent into using.

## Three things worth knowing

**Nothing is allowed until you allow it.** A new agent cannot spend a cent.
Every permission is one you granted, on purpose.

**Refusals are kept, not dropped.** When Pocket stops a purchase it records what
was asked for, what stopped it, and how much room was left. Weeks later you can
still answer "why did my agent stop?".

**Your agent never holds a key.** It asks Pocket to pay. It cannot sign for
itself, so a leaked prompt cannot spend anything.

## Works with what you already run

Hermes, Claude Code, Codex, or anything else that speaks MCP. One command
connects it, and there is no key in that command.
